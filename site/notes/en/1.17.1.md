## 1.17.1: the unmute sound is now the high one

- **The microphone and headphone sounds were swapped.** Muting sounded higher than unmuting, the opposite of what you'd expect. Now the high sound means open: unmuting and undeafening sound higher, muting and deafening sound lower. This matters most for anyone who uses the app by ear, without looking at the screen.
- The sounds for turning the camera on and off, and for being muted by a moderator, are the same as the microphone's and changed along with it.
