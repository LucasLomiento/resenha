## 1.11.0: Spotify in the call, volume up to 200% and more

- **Spotify bot:** the music note button, in the bottom panel, brings your Spotify into the call. It shows up as one more person in the room (in the voice channel list and as a tile on the call screen, with the cover art) and plays for everyone, in stereo.
- **Everyone at their own volume:** click Spotify inside the voice channel and set its volume just for you. Deafening silences the bot too.
- **Anyone can control it:** play, pause, skip, go back, shuffle, repeat, drag the position, search for songs, playlists or albums, add to the queue and play one of the playlists of whoever added the bot.
- **Needs Spotify Premium** for whoever adds the bot (listeners don't need anything) and, for now, only works on Linux. The first time, the panel installs `librespot` (the Spotify player the bot uses under the hood; it asks for your password) and connects your account through the browser. The Spotify app doesn't even need to be open.

## Also in this version

- **Someone's volume up to 200%:** in the voice channel, click the person and go past 100%, like on Discord. The sound is amplified inside Resenha: the system volume doesn't change.
- **Switch rooms with a double click:** in Settings → Voice & video → Call. With it on, if you're already in a call you need two clicks on another voice channel to switch (a single click just warns you), so nobody leaves the conversation by accident.
- **Rooms through Cloudflare per person:** the Resenha owner can let other accounts route a voice channel through Cloudflare, like he does. It's in Settings → Platform → Accounts (each person's cloud icon), and it turns on or off instantly.
