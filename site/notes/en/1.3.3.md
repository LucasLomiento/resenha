## 1.3.3: streaming no longer weighs on your game

- **Performance fix:** since 1.2, just sharing your screen kept the doodle layer open over the monitor the whole time, even with nobody doodling. With a layer on top of the game, Hyprland loses game mode (tearing and direct scanout) and starts compositing everything every frame, which cost performance. Now the layer only shows up while there are doodles on screen and goes away on its own right after. Same goes for Windows.
