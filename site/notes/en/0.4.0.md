## What's new

- **Webcam in calls.** Turn it on with the camera button in the call panel. The call screen (click "Voice connected" or the voice channel) shows a tile per person: the video of whoever has their camera on, the avatar of whoever doesn't, a green outline on whoever's talking and the **Watch** button for whoever's sharing their screen. The camera runs at 720p 30 fps. You can pick which camera and see a preview in Settings → Voice.
- **The tray icon lights up green while you talk**, and still switches to muted and deafened. On Windows, the badge on the taskbar icon does the same.

On an older version? Update both sides so the webcam shows up properly.
