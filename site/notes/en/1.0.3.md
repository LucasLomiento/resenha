## 1.0.3 — Stereo screen audio

- **Stream audio now arrives in real stereo.** It was already sent in stereo by whoever shares, but the viewer's app merged both sides into one. Now left and right arrive separately (games, music and videos with the sound in the right place), at 128 kbps. Call voice stays the same.

Added in 1.0.2: the call and the stream survive connection drops without anyone leaving the room.
