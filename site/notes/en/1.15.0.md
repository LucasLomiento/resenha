## 1.15.0: full review (dozens of fixes)

A review of the whole app, from the server to the phone. What changes most day to day:

**Privacy**
- **Invisible and Do not disturb really apply to your friends.** When you opened the app (or picked your status on the phone with the computer open), friends could see you online until the connection dropped.
- **A voice message goes to the conversation it was recorded in**, even if you switch conversations while it converts.
- **Camera and screen share don't stay on by themselves:** if the call ended while the screen picker or the camera was opening, the capture kept going. Double-clicking the camera also no longer leaves the light on.
- On the phone, your login no longer goes into the Google backup.

**Calls and private calls**
- **No more getting logged out on your own right after a reconnect** (sometimes the app thought the session had expired).
- **Private calls:** the ringtone doesn't keep ringing forever with two calls at the same time; the call shows up when you open the app from the phone notification; hanging up while the internet was dropping doesn't leave you "in the call" for the other person; joining from the phone takes the computer out of the call, with a notice.
- **Full channel, no permission or deleted channel:** the app no longer sits in a ghost call (join sound, mic open and nobody listening).
- Without permission to speak in the channel, nobody hears the person (before, only the muted icon showed up).
- A mic that disconnects mid-call (headset out of battery) falls back to the default, with a notice. With no mic at all, you can join just to listen.

**Chat**
- **Right-click menu:** copy, paste, copy link and spell-check suggestions.
- **Link previews no longer lose their image** after a few days.
- The "New" line shows up when you open the conversation, and jumping to the replied message works in private messages too.
- Esc closes the @, # and : suggestions without messing up the text; the draft doesn't vanish when you open Friends; "React" from the right-click menu opens the picker; "Copy text" keeps code as it was written.
- A message that got no confirmation during a drop is sent again on its own, without duplicating.
- Emojis with English and Spanish names in search.
- System notifications show names instead of codes and hide spoilers.

**Phone**
- With the app open, a new message no longer turns into a system notification on top.
- The "In call" notification goes away when you close the app from recents.

**Computer**
- Doodles work again on fresh Arch and CachyOS installs (a package was missing).
- On GNOME without the tray extension, closing the window no longer hides the app forever.
- Component licenses in Settings → App.

And lots of work on the server: fairer limits against abuse, a Cloudflare media quota that doesn't overflow, and lower resource usage.
