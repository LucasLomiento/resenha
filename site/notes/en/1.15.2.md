## 1.15.2: louder voice on Windows

- **On Windows, the mic's automatic gain control now comes on by default**: there, the mic reaches the app without the volume boost the driver usually gives, and your voice came out much quieter than on Discord. Anyone already using the app on Windows gets it turned on with this update, except those who picked the Studio profile. You can turn it off in Settings → Voice & video → Automatic gain control. Nothing changed on Linux.
