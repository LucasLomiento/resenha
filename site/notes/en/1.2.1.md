## 1.2.1: the doodle pencil shows up on Linux

- **Fix:** on Linux (Wayland: Hyprland, KDE), the doodle pencil didn't show up for viewers, even with the entire monitor shared. The system reports every capture as a "window"; now the app looks at the shape of the streamed image.
- **The right monitor on its own:** with two monitors of the same size, the app figures out which one you're streaming. A small pink square flashes for a moment in the top-left corner when the stream starts: that's the app looking for it. If it can't tell, it asks in the "Live" panel.
