## What's new

- **Noise reduction with RNNoise**, the same filter as noise-suppression-for-voice. Works the same on Linux and Windows, with nothing to set up on your system. It removes keyboard, fan and background noise. It's in Settings → Voice, where you can choose between RNNoise, Chromium's filter or none. If your mic already goes through a filter on your system, leave it off here.
- **Mic threshold** (optional): only transmits when you're talking. There's **automatic** mode, where RNNoise recognizes your voice, and **manual** mode, where you drag the threshold along a live meter. It opens instantly and holds for a moment after you stop talking, so the end of your words doesn't get cut off.
- **Test microphone** now goes through the same filter and threshold as the call, so you hear exactly what the others will hear.
