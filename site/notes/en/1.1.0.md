## 1.1.0 — Map together, custom profiles and new sounds

### Server map (everyone together)
- **"Map" at the top of the channel list** (and a "Map" button on the call card): everyone who opens it sees the same place. Whoever drags or zooms takes the others along.
- **Live cursors** with each person's name, **pins** with a label and color that stay saved on the server, **address search** and the **"Google Maps"** button, which opens the same place in the browser (for satellite and Street View).
- Dark, light or colorful map, each person's choice. Maps come from OpenFreeMap and search from OpenStreetMap (no account, no key).

### Your profile, your way (free, for everyone)
- **Banner** (image or GIF) and **animated avatar** (GIF: animates on the card and on hover).
- Two-color **card theme**, **pronouns**, **avatar frame** (neon, aurora, headphones, little stars, flames, flowers, crown, kitty) and **card effect** (confetti, snow, sparkles, hearts, bubbles, fireflies).
- **Styled name:** font (classic, rounded, cursive, pixel) and effect (gradient or neon), in chat, in the member list and on the card.
- All in Settings → Profile → Customize, with a live preview.

### New sounds
- Each kind of event has its own tone: joining and leaving the call, others joining, mute, deafen, message, **mention** (now different from a message), going live and the stream ending.
- **A sound when someone starts or stops watching your screen**, plus the avatars of who's watching in the stream panel.
- All volumes evened out. You can listen to each one in Settings → Notifications.

### And more
- **Call screen:** everyone's photo shows up (before, just the initial) and the tiles grow to fill the screen.
- The **bottom card** (call and you) now reaches the left corner.
- **Ctrl+/** shows all the shortcuts.
