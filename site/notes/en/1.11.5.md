## 1.11.5: genres on Spotify

- **Each song's genre:** in the Spotify panel, the song playing and the search results show the artist's genre in a tag next to the name (sertanejo, mpb, rock...).
- **Search by genre:** click a tag to see songs from that genre, or pick one of the ready-made genres that show up when the search is empty. You can type it too: `genre:"funk carioca"`.
- The genre comes from the artist on Spotify, which doesn't classify everyone (especially new artists): for those, the tag doesn't show up.
