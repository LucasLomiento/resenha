## 1.13.3: Spotify on Windows

- **The Spotify bot now works on Windows:** the music note button shows up in the call and the Spotify you add plays for everyone, like on Linux. On Windows, Resenha already comes with everything the bot needs (nothing to install): just create your Spotify app (the panel shows how) and connect. Requires Spotify Premium.
- First version on Windows: if something doesn't work, let us know with a screenshot.
