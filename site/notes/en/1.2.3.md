## 1.2.3: doodles without seeing double, and an option to allow them

- **No doubled doodles:** whoever doodles no longer sees their own stroke twice (theirs, instantly, and the one coming back late through the stream). The app draws your stroke exactly on top of the copy arriving through the video, so you only see yours, with no delay. Other viewers still see your doodles through the stream.
- **The sharer decides whether people can doodle:** the "Let viewers doodle" option now also shows up when you start sharing and in Settings → Voice & video (besides the "Live" panel). With it off, viewers see a grayed-out pencil saying you didn't allow it.
- **Details:** the name of whoever's drawing stays put at the start of the stroke; "Clear doodles" also clears them on the drawer's screen; the quick click (to point at something) shows only through the stream.
