## 1.3.1: doodles always on the screen being streamed

- **No picking a monitor:** doodles always go to the monitor you're sharing. The "Left | Right" in the "Live" panel and the notice asking you to choose are gone. The app finds the right monitor on its own; if it can't, doodles stay off for that stream (instead of showing up on the wrong screen).
- **System notifications open Resenha:** clicking a message, call or update notification now brings the window to the front, even with the app hidden in the tray.
