## 1.11.3: the Spotify bot joins the call

- **"Add Spotify to the call" works again:** in 1.11.2 the bot logged into your account, but Spotify wouldn't let it become a device (the "That didn't work" error). In August Spotify changed how it accepts this kind of player, and Resenha now logs in the new way. Tested with a real account.
- **If you already connected on 1.11.2**, you don't need to do anything: the first time you add the bot, the browser asks you to confirm Spotify again (just once), and that's it.
