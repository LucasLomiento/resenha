## 1.14.2: calls and notifications on the phone

- **Calls from the phone:** anyone who joined a voice channel from their phone showed up as talking, but nobody could hear them, and sometimes "Slow down! Too many actions at once" showed up. Fixed: the phone's sound reaches everyone in the call (and everyone's sound reaches the phone).
- **Phone notifications with the computer on:** before, just having Resenha open on the computer was enough for the phone to stay quiet. Now the phone only stays quiet while you're **looking** at Resenha on the computer. Walked away or switched to another window: the phone notifies you.
- **Test notification:** in Settings → Notifications, the phone shows whether Android is letting Resenha notify you and has a button to send a test notification.
- Notices on the phone screen ("Slow down!...", errors) no longer come out squished against the edge.
- In chat, the time that shows when you hover over a message no longer wraps onto two lines (in English, "9:58 PM").
