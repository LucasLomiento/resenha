## 1.9.0: Resenha in English and Spanish

- **Three languages:** Portuguese, English and Spanish, across the whole app: screens, menus, notices, system notifications, the tray and the server's error messages. Change it in Settings → App → Language; the interface switches instantly, with no restart and without dropping you from the call.
- **Automatic:** new installs start in the system language (English if it's none of these three). Anyone already using the app stays in Portuguese.
- Dates, times and numbers follow the chosen language, and the settings search (and Ctrl+K) finds options typed in any of the three.
- The "Test microphone" button no longer cuts off its text when it's longer, and the device list shows device names in the chosen language.
