## 1.18.1: one message can no longer freeze anyone's chat

- **One message can no longer freeze or break the chat.** Certain messages (lots of emoji followed by text, thousands of asterisks, or a code block crafted on purpose) froze the app for anyone opening the channel, or made the channel stop showing messages. Fixed, and if a message fails to render, only that one falls back to plain text.
- **Read only when you actually saw it.** On your phone, tapping a server no longer marks its last channel as read without opening it. On your computer, reconnecting with the call or the map on screen no longer marks the channel behind it.
- **Polls**: quickly clicking two options (with multiple answers) no longer drops one of the votes.
- **Chat**: opening a channel with new messages jumps to the "New" line on the very first open; a channel with few messages loads older ones even without a scrollbar; in a direct message, jumping to an old message no longer leaves a gap when a new one arrives; the unread dot no longer gets stuck after a ban that deletes messages.
- **Updating on Linux without a password agent**: the terminal that asks for your password closed with an error and the app didn't come back. Now it installs and reopens, even if you close the terminal.
- **Push to talk**: if the key gets another use in your system while the app is open, the Shortcuts page shows it right away, and the mic tooltip reminds you which key to hold.
- **Moved to another room** by a moderator: audio with everyone comes back right away instead of after a few seconds.
- **Fewer requests to the server** (friends' presence, read markers, status) and more small fixes: logging out mid-call clears the tray, and closing the window while streaming (on Windows) no longer leaves the app running hidden.
