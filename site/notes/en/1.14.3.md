## 1.14.3: friends, text box and streams

- **Clicking a friend opens your conversation with them** (in the Friends list). Their profile is still in the row's "⋯" menu.
- **Text box:** after sending a multi-line message, the box goes back to its normal size, and the conversation stays at the bottom (before, it stayed big and the last messages were hidden).
- **Streams:** the controls hide on their own after a few seconds. To bring them back, move the mouse to the bottom of the stream, or move the mouse out and back in. In full screen the cursor hides too.
