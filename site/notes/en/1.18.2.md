## 1.18.2: the mic only in the tray, with new icons

- **KDE (and the rest of Linux):** during a call, the mic no longer shows up in the window's title bar or in Alt+Tab. On KDE the window icon is a single one (title, Alt+Tab and taskbar), and there's no badge just for the taskbar button like on Windows, so the call status lives in Resenha's icon in the system tray, on the panel at the bottom.
- **New status icons**, in Discord's style, in the tray and on the Windows taskbar badge: a dark circle with a green mic during a call, a green ring around it while you talk, and the mic or the headphones crossed out in red when you're muted or deafened.
