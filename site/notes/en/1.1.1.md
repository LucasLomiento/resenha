## 1.1.1 — Street View on the map

- **Google Street View inside the map:** the little person button in the map bar (then click the spot) or "Street View" on a pin's card. It opens next to the map.
- **Together with the crew:** whoever's on the map sees a little person with your name where you're looking (and which way you're facing). Click it to go to the same place.
- **Fix:** muting and unmuting a stream goes back to the volume it was at, instead of jumping to max.
