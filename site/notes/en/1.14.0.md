## 1.14.0: Resenha on your phone

- **Android app:** download the APK from the website (Download → Android). It has everything for day-to-day use: servers, private conversations, friends, voice and video calls, watching whoever's sharing their screen, the map and listening to (and controlling) the call's Spotify. You can't share your screen or add Spotify from the phone yet.
- **The call keeps going with the screen off:** there's an "In call" notification with Mute and Leave buttons. On the call screen there are mic, headphones, camera (plus the one to flip to the back camera) and leave buttons.
- **Notifications on your phone, even with the app closed:** private messages, calls and server messages, following what you picked for each one (all, only mentions, nothing). If you're using your computer, the phone stays quiet.
- **Installing:** requires Android 7 or newer. Since it's not from the Play Store, the first time Android asks you to allow installs from the browser. After that, the app lets you know when a new version is out.
