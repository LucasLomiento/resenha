## 1.17.0: Resenha holds up against a misbehaving app

- **One misbehaving app no longer takes Resenha down for everyone.** On Oct 25 a direct call got stuck in a loop and used up the server's entire daily limit; everything was down until midnight UTC. Now the app itself caps what it sends, the server ends a call that gets stuck in a loop ("The call ran into a problem and was ended. Call again." shows up), and anyone sending too much is paused for a few minutes without affecting anyone else.
- **Steadier calls**: when both sides rebuilt the connection at the same time, the call sometimes went silent for both; not anymore. And a connection that can't go direct retries calmly instead of nonstop.
- **Lighter map**: moving the map on your own sends nothing to others (just where you stop), and with more people it sends fewer updates per second, still moving smoothly.
- **For whoever runs the platform**: Settings → Platform shows today's requests to the server, who used the most (by name), the apps the server held back and each account's app version. Near the limit, the owner gets a notification and open sign-up pauses on its own until the day resets.
- Someone who closes their laptop or loses their connection without leaving the app may show as online for a few extra minutes.
