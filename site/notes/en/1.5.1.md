## 1.5.1: updating mid-call doesn't kick you out of the room

- **Back to the room after updating:** if you click "Restart and update" in the middle of a call, you're taken back to the same room when the app reopens, with your mic and sound the way they were. In a private call, you go back if the other person is still in it (it never calls again on its own). Screen share and camera don't come back on their own: just share again.
