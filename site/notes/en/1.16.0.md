## 1.16.0: call status in the tray and notifications that clear

- **The tray icon shows your call, like on Discord**: in a call with your mic on it turns green and lights up while you talk; muted or deafened, it turns red with the mic or headphones crossed out. On Windows the same icon shows as a badge on the taskbar icon.
- **Seen on one device, the notification clears from the others**: read (or reply to) a message on your computer and its notification leaves your phone; read it on your phone and it leaves your computer (on Windows, even from the notification center). This works for direct messages and server messages, and the "is calling you" notification clears when you answer somewhere else or the call ends.
- **On the phone**, the app left open on a conversation in the background marked incoming messages there as read; it doesn't anymore. And if you installed the app from 1.15.0 on, it never asked for permission to notify and notifications didn't pop up on screen: now the app asks when it opens and notifications show up as they should.
