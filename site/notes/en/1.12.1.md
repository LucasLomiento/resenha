## 1.12.1: direct rooms are fully direct

- **Direct rooms and private calls are now P2P for everything:** the screen share goes directly to each person, no matter how many are watching, and Cloudflare doesn't even step in as a fallback when the direct connection fails. If it can't connect directly with someone, the app lets you know and suggests routing the room through Cloudflare.
- **Rooms through Cloudflare keep everything through Cloudflare:** voice, camera and screen share, and a screen share with 2 or more watching goes up only once to the server there. Turning it on or off mid-call switches everyone's path instantly (the sound drops for a second).
