## 1.10.0: shortcuts with the app in the background and a smarter dock

- **Shortcuts work outside the app on Hyprland:** mute, deafen, share screen, leave the call and show the window now work with another window focused. Resenha creates the shortcuts in Hyprland by itself while it's open (they show up in the system's shortcut list). If the key is already used for something else in your config, it leaves it alone and lets you know in Settings → Shortcuts.
- **Your photo turns green when you talk**, in the dock at the bottom too, even with a frame (before, the frame covered the ring).
- **Little arrow on the dock's mic and headphones:** pick your microphone and audio output without opening settings, plus the mic profile: **Voice isolation** (cuts noise and echo) or **Studio** (pure sound, no processing at all, for headphone users). Right-clicking the mic or headphones opens the same menu.
