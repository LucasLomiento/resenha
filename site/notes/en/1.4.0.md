## 1.4.0: screen share through Cloudflare when several people watch

- **Streaming to two or more people is much lighter:** before, each viewer cost a whole video encode and the full upload again (at 1440p60, one more person is ~12 Mbps and more CPU taken away from the game). Now, when the second person asks to watch, the screen goes up only once to Cloudflare's media server (SFU), which distributes it to whoever's watching. With just one viewer it still goes direct, like before, with the lowest delay. The switch doesn't flicker: the direct copy only stops after the image through Cloudflare has arrived. Delay goes up a little (about 15 ms in testing). The stream stats show "via Cloudflare".
- **Connections that can't go direct:** when someone's network doesn't allow a direct connection (some ISPs with CGNAT), the call goes through Cloudflare's relay (TURN) instead of failing.
- **No cost:** Cloudflare gives 1,000 GB a month for free across both. The apps count what goes through, and Resenha stops using Cloudflare at 850 GB; then everything goes direct again until the month rolls over. In Settings → Platform, the owner sees how much has been used.
- **Privacy:** through TURN, the content passes encrypted and Cloudflare sees nothing. Through the SFU (only for screen shares with 2 or more watching), the path is encrypted, but the server opens the image and sound to forward them, like every server of this kind; nothing is recorded. Voice always stays direct.
- Anyone on an old version keeps getting the screen directly.
