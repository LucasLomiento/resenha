## 1.8.1: the best friend badge

- **Pioneer badge with new text:** hovering over the badge shows "Best friend of Resenha's owner, the most badass dude I've ever met. Sometimes way too pissed off, sometimes way too much of a bitch." In Settings → Platform it shows up as "Best friend".
- **New icon on the badge:** instead of the flag, a white heart with little red devil horns, in the same blue medallion with a silver rim.
- **The best friend sees Platform:** in Settings → Platform he sees the numbers for all of Resenha (accounts, servers, active invites, used space and media through Cloudflare this month), read-only. Sign-ups, the account list and suspensions stay with the owner. The panel now also shows how many invites are still valid.
