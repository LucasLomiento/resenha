## 1.13.2: drag to switch rooms

- **Move people by dragging:** anyone who can move people in a call (through their role) can now grab someone in the voice channel list and drop them in another room. The target room lights up while you drag. Without the permission, you can't drag anyone.
- **No more blue "#"** above the title at the start of each channel.
- **Only what works on your system shows up:** on Windows, the button to add Spotify to the call no longer appears (the bot only runs on Linux). Spotify that someone else added still shows up in the call so you can listen, change its volume and control it. The sound option in "Share screen" also disappears when the system has no way to capture sound.
