## 1.7.0: "Reconnect?" after updating, and the Pioneer

- **Back to the call after updating:** if you update in the middle of a call, when the app opens you see a pill at the top: "You were in General · Crew before updating", with **Reconnect** (back in with your mic and sound as they were) and **Not now**. Updating from 1.5.1 to 1.6.0 this failed because 1.6.0 looked for the saved call in the wrong place. Now it's found in both places.
- **Pioneer:** the first person who joined Resenha after the owner gets the **Pioneer** badge, with a planted flag. They also get the "Pioneer" frame (a sea ring, a comet around it and the flag waving), the "Horizon" name (a sunrise over the sea running across the letters), their own sound when joining a call and a silver and blue shine in the list. They're theirs alone. The owner checks and, if needed, changes who it is in Settings → Platform.
