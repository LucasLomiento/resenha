## 1.18.0: polls, push to talk and colored code

- **Polls in channels.** The new button next to the paperclip opens "New poll": the question, 2 to 10 options, one answer or several, and a deadline (or none). Everyone sees the result right away and who voted for what; whoever created it (or a moderator) can end it anytime.
- **Push to talk.** In Settings → Shortcuts, pick your "Talk key": your mic only opens while you hold it, even with a game focused. It works on Linux with Wayland (Hyprland, KDE, GNOME); where the system doesn't allow it, the option doesn't even show up.
- **Colored code in chat.** A code block with a language (` ```js `, `py`, `sh`, `json`…) gets syntax colors, and hovering it shows a copy button.
- **Steadier calls.** A repeated answer from the connection could leave a call renegotiating nonstop; not anymore. Leaving a call while your internet was down no longer leaves you "stuck" in the room list. Answering right after clicking a room works, and answering a call that already ended doesn't call back on its own.
- **Camera and screen follow the room's permission**: without video permission the buttons are greyed out (before, the camera turned on and nobody saw it).
- **Your phone notifies you right away** even if your computer went to sleep with Resenha open (it could hold the notification for about 6 minutes).
- **Messages after a drop**: the automatic resend no longer messes up your unreads, and a message with only an image doesn't come back with "the attachment is no longer available". Voice messages retry before giving up instead of losing the recording.
- **A leaner server that's harder to take down**: fewer internal calls per message and per reconnection, and several new brakes against apps stuck in a loop and against anyone trying to burn the daily limit on purpose.
- Anyone still on 1.17 in a room that goes through Cloudflare sees streams over the direct connection until they update.
