## 1.13.0: a new look

- **New look across the whole app:** graphite background, electric blue on buttons and on whatever's selected, and green only for who's talking, online or in a call. Headings got their own font, buttons got rounder and the app icon turned blue.
- **The Resenha mascot:** the little face from the icon shows up on the sign-in screen and on the Home button, on the left, and laughs when you hover over it.
- **New sign-in screen:** "Log in" and "Create account" tabs and, next to them, an animated showcase of the app. With open sign-ups, the screen no longer jumps back to "Create account" on its own every few seconds.
- **Roomier settings:** the page fills the window, with the menu on the left. Voice & video now has Audio, Voice processing, Camera, Connection, Streaming and Advanced; App has Language and appearance, Behavior, When the computer starts, Window and Updates. The settings search (and Ctrl+K) finds everything in the new places.
- **Bigger Spotify panel:** the player with the big cover art on one side, the tabs and search on the other. When you scroll the list, the search bar no longer covers the songs.
- **Notices in the middle of the screen:** they sit above the message box, the same notice doesn't show up twice and, with a dialog open, they move to the top.
- **New website:** [the Resenha website](https://lucaslomiento.github.io/resenha/) is now a replica of the app you can click around: each channel shows a part (call, screen share, doodles, map, profile, Spotify, internet drops), with what's new and the downloads.
