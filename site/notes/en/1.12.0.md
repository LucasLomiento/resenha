## 1.12.0: the shortest path in the call

- **"Connecting…" only when it really drops:** Cloudflare moves the server connection around from time to time, for everyone. The app already came back on its own without kicking anyone out of the call; now it comes back instantly, without a notice, and "Connecting…" only shows up if it's out for more than 4 seconds.
- **The chat doesn't flicker on reconnect:** messages stay on screen (without losing your place) and whatever arrived in the meantime shows up.
- **Best path for direct connections:** when both PCs have IPv4 and IPv6, the app measures the ping on both and, if one is much lower, the call switches to it on its own (once, with the sound dropping for a second). Nothing goes through Cloudflare: a direct room stays direct. You can turn it off in Settings → Voice & video → Call.
- **Turning on Cloudflare for a room mid-call now applies right away:** until now, whoever was already in the call stayed direct until they left and rejoined. Now the connection is rebuilt through Cloudflare (the sound drops for a second).
- **Which way the call goes:** hovering over someone in the call, the ping tooltip shows whether the connection goes over IPv4 or IPv6, and the ping over the other path when it can be measured.
- **Updating with the Spotify bot on** no longer leaves its player (librespot) running hidden after the app closes.
