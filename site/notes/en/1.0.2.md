## 1.0.2 — The call survives connection drops

- **Lost your connection for a few seconds? The call keeps going.** Nobody leaves the room for the others, and whoever was watching your screen keeps watching, without clicking again. Voice and screen go straight between the PCs, so they keep running while the app reconnects.
- **Not even the server restarting kills the stream:** the screen share doesn't stop and the call comes back on its own.
- **Private calls too:** dropping and coming back no longer hangs up.
- **The app notices the drop much faster** (about 10 s, it used to take up to 70 s) and reconnects right away.
