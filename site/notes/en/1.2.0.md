## 1.2.0: doodles on the screen

- **Doodle on the sharer's screen** (like in Slack huddles): while watching a stream, click the pencil in the player and draw on top. The person sharing sees the doodles right on their monitor, on top of everything (even fullscreen games), with your name and color, and viewers see them through the stream itself.
  - **Laser** (the stroke fades in under a second) or **Pen** (stays for a few seconds). A quick click marks a spot ("look here"). Esc exits doodle mode.
  - Only works when sharing the **entire monitor**. Each person's resolution doesn't matter.
  - The sharer can **clear** or **turn off** doodles in the stream panel (the "Live" button). With more than one monitor, the app tries to figure out which one is being streamed; if it can't, it asks there.
  - On Linux, the package now installs `gtk4-layer-shell` and `python-gobject` (used to draw on top of the screen). Works on Hyprland and KDE; not on GNOME.
- **Fix:** when completing an @mention with Tab, fast typers sometimes typed before the cursor jumped.
