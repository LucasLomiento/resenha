## What's new

- **Updates from inside the app.** When a new version is out, **Update** shows up at the top of the sidebar (or in Settings → App). On Arch/CachyOS it asks for your password to install the `.pacman`.
- **Private messages.** In the **Direct messages** section, click someone: only the two of you can see the conversation.
- **Custom shortcuts** (Settings → Shortcuts): mute, deafen, share screen, leave the call and show Resenha. They work with the app minimized.
- **Sounds** when joining and leaving the call, muting, deafening, when someone starts streaming and when a message arrives. You can turn them off and adjust the volume.
- **Tray icon** (Windows, KDE and others), with mute, deafen and leave call. On Windows a badge also shows up on the taskbar icon when you're muted.
- **Open when your computer starts**, optional, hidden in the tray.
- **Quality and codec change live**, no need to stop the stream: click the screen icon in the call panel. It also shows how many people are watching.
- **The stream thumbnail** can be dragged and resized, and there's the system's **floating window**, which stays on top of everything.
- **Mic test** now plays your voice back in your headphones.
- **Images are compressed** before sending (WebP, up to 2560 px).
- **Controls always in the same place**, at any screen size, plus **interface zoom** (Ctrl + / Ctrl − / Ctrl 0).
- **New icon.**
- Fixed: the chat wouldn't scroll.
- The server is automatic now: it no longer shows up on the login screen.
