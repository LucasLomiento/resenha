## 1.11.1: Spotify with nothing to set up

- **Connecting Spotify is now one click:** in the Spotify panel (music note button, in the bottom panel), **Connect** opens the browser, you authorize it and you're done. Nothing needs to be configured on the server anymore.
- **Improve the connection (optional):** if you want, create an app on the Spotify website (about 2 minutes, requires Premium) and paste the Client ID into the panel. The bot's requests then use a quota of your own, with less chance of "too many requests". The step-by-step is right in the panel.

## In 1.11.0

- **Spotify bot:** brings your Spotify into the call. It shows up as one more person in the room and plays for everyone, in stereo; each person sets its volume just for themselves, and anyone can control it (play, pause, skip, search songs/playlists/albums, queue, playlists of whoever added it). Needs Spotify Premium for whoever adds the bot and, for now, only works on Linux.
- **Someone's volume up to 200%**, amplified inside Resenha (the system volume doesn't change).
- **Switch rooms with a double click** (Settings → Voice & video → Call).
- **Rooms through Cloudflare per person:** the owner chooses who can, in Settings → Platform → Accounts.
