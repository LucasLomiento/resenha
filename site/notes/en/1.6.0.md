## 1.6.0: voice messages

- **Voice messages:** the mic in the message box records (up to 15 minutes). Enter or the paper plane sends, Esc or the trash can discards. It goes out as an **.mp3**, so you can download it and play it anywhere. In chat, a player shows up with the audio waveform (click it to skip), the time, the speed (1×, 1.5× and 2×) and the download button. Any audio file sent in chat gets the same player.
- **Getting back to the room after updating, more reliably:** the room you were in is now saved to a file before the app closes to install. Before, it could get lost when the app closed too quickly. Works starting with the next update (whoever clicks update needs to already be on this version).
