## 1.0 — Multiple servers, friends and a new look

The biggest update so far: Resenha stops being a single group and becomes a full app, Discord-style ([roadmap](https://github.com/LucasLomiento/resenha/blob/main/docs/ROADMAP.md)). **Nothing gets lost:** your group became the first server, with all its messages, files and accounts, and anyone already logged in stays logged in.

### Servers
- **Create as many servers as you want**, with a name and icon. Invite by link, with an expiry date and a usage limit (the link opens straight in the app).
- **Roles** with colors and permissions, **private channels**, categories; organize channels by dragging them.
- **Moderation:** kick, ban, time out, mute, deafen or move someone in the call, per-server nicknames and a log of everything the moderators did.

### Friends and conversations
- **Home** with friends (request, accept, block) and **private messages**, with **voice and video calls** right in the conversation.
- **Profile:** username, display name, photo, "about me" and color.
- **Status:** online, idle (automatic, when you're away from the PC), do not disturb, invisible and custom status.

### Messages
- Discord-style formatting, **@mentions** (person, role, @everyone) and **#channels**.
- Reply, react with emoji, pin and **search** (no need to worry about accents).
- Link previews generated by the server: the site never sees your IP.
- Unreads and mentions counted per channel and server; notifications adjustable per server and per channel.
- **Ctrl+K** to jump to any channel or conversation, **↑** to edit your last message.

### Account and privacy
- Connected devices (log out of one or all), change password, delete account and **download a copy of your data**.
- Privacy policy and terms of use.
- Full security review: 15 issues found and fixed, each with a test.

### New look
Interface redesigned from scratch: dark, clean, with less text and things always in the same place.
