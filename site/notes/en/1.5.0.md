## 1.5.0: the Resenha owner looks the part

- **Founder badge:** a medallion with the Resenha logo next to the owner's name everywhere (messages, member list, profile card, call list). There's only one, and the server decides who gets it.
- **"Founder" frame:** a ring in Resenha's colors with golden laurels and the logo medallion at the bottom. Only the owner can use it.
- **Holographic name:** a shine that runs across the letters of the name. Also owner-only.
- **Call entrance:** when the owner joins a call, a short fanfare plays instead of the usual sound and his name enters the list with a shine.
- The frame and the name stay still in lists and chat and only animate on hover, to keep things light.
