## 1.13.1: pick your camera

- **Pick your camera:** with more than one camera, the "Camera" button opens a picker, like when sharing your screen: each one shows up live and the one you chose comes already selected next time (it becomes the camera in settings). Double-clicking a camera also turns it on. With just one camera, it turns on directly like before.
- **Spotify:** with long artist names (wrapping onto more than one line), the volume disappeared under the player. Now the cover art shrinks when space runs out and the volume always shows.
