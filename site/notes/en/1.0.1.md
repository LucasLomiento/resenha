## 1.0.1 — Dark icon and a tray you can spot from afar

- **New icon, dark like the app.** Out goes the colorful gradient; in comes the laughing speech bubble with a headset, in violet on a dark background.
- **Muted or deafened, the whole tray icon changes:** it turns into a red square with a crossed-out mic or headphones, easy to see even at the tray's tiny size. When you talk, it still turns green. On KDE, the taskbar icon changes too.

Added in 1.0: multiple servers, friends and private messages, roles and moderation, a new look ([1.0 notes](https://github.com/LucasLomiento/resenha/releases/tag/v1.0.0)).
