## 1.11.2: Spotify actually connecting

- **Connecting Spotify works again:** in 1.11.1 the login opened, but Spotify answered "too many requests". Search and controls now go through your own Spotify app, which has a quota just for you.
- **The panel shows how to create the app** (about 2 minutes, just once, requires Premium): open the Spotify developer dashboard, create the app, register the address `http://127.0.0.1:5589/spotify` (there's a copy button) and paste the Client ID. Then just **Connect**: the browser asks you to confirm twice the first time (your app and Spotify).
- **If Spotify asks for a break**, Resenha waits as long as it says instead of insisting.
