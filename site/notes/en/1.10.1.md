## 1.10.1: shortcuts with the app in the background on KDE

- **Shortcuts outside the app on KDE Plasma (Wayland):** since KDE 6.7.4, mute, deafen and the other shortcuts only worked the first time Resenha opened. Now the app registers the shortcuts with the system every time it opens, and they work again with another window focused. If you change a shortcut's key, KDE may ask you to confirm once.
