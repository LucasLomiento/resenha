## 1.2.2: settings search and updates that don't fail

- **Settings search:** a field at the top of the list (or Ctrl+F). Type it your way, without accents, with typos or with the name you know from Discord ("avatar", "webcam", "dm", "zoom", "noise"…), and Enter takes you straight to the option, which flashes on screen.
- **Settings in Ctrl+K too:** "echo", "tray", "password" and the app opens right at the option.
- **New version notice:** the app checks for updates every 30 minutes, downloads them on its own (only outside calls, so the stream doesn't stutter) and shows a notice in the corner when you can restart and update. "What's new" opens what changed.
- **Fix (Linux):** sometimes, after clicking "Restart and update", the app closed without installing, and sometimes it didn't even reopen. It always happened when it had been reopened by the previous update: Linux left that app without permission to ask for the admin password. Now installing and reopening go through systemd, without that lock.

**Heads-up for this update:** the fix kicks in from the next one. If the app closes without updating this time, open it again and click update, and it'll work.
